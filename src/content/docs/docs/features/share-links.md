---
title: Share links
description: Publish read-only public pages for a lure, spot or catch, and revoke them at any time.
---

**Share links** let you publish a single **lure**, **spot** or **catch** as a read-only public
page: handy for showing off a catch or sharing a lure with a friend without giving them access
to your whole instance.

:::note
Share links need login. With `ADMIN_PASSWORD` set (see [Multi-user & authentication](/docs/multi-user/authentication)), every lure, spot and catch shows
**Create share link**; without login it isn't there.
:::

## Create a share link

1. Open the lure, spot or catch.
2. Select **Create share link**, then **Copy** and send the link.
3. To take the page down again, select **Remove link**.

The link opens a **read-only** page with just that item; the rest of your data stays private. It is
built from your `BASE_URL`, so a home-network address only opens on your home network.
